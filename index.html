How to get the train
  1. Follow signs for AirTrain at your terminal ("Rail Station / NJ Transit"). It runs continuously — no need to time it.
  2. Ride to the Newark Liberty Airport Rail Station stop.
  3. Buy an NJ Transit ticket at a vending machine or the NJ Transit app (app avoids a $5 surcharge). AirTrain fee is included.
  4. Board any Northeast Corridor or North Jersey Coast Line train toward New York Penn Station.
  5. Ride is about 30 minutes. Keep your ticket — you'll show it again at the AirTrain gate.
  6. Arrive at Penn Station (31st & 8th Ave) — short walk or cab to the Doubletree.
